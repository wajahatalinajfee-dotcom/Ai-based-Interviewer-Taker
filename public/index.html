FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it free?

You can start for free. Interviews use a free AI tier, so at busy times you may briefly see a "try again" message.

Do I need a microphone?

Voice works best in Chrome or Edge with a microphone. You can always type your answers instead.

What data do you keep?

We store your account, scores and reports so your dashboard works. We do not store audio. Your answers are sent to Google's Gemini API to be scored, and Chrome's speech recognition sends audio to Google.

Will I get the same questions twice?

No. Each question is generated fresh from a random topic, type and scenario, and recent questions are blocked.

Which roles are supported?

Programming basics, MERN, MEAN and AI developer tracks today, with more roles planned.
